Privacy Policy

W is a calorie, training and nutrition app. This page explains what data it uses and why.

Data on your phone

There are no accounts. Your profile (including any profile picture), food log, workouts, weigh-ins, water and steps are stored only on your phone. A backup file is made only when you choose Back up to a file, and goes wherever you save or share it.

Health data

If you allow it, the app reads your step count and weight from Apple Health or Health Connect, and saves weigh-ins you log back to them. Health data is used only to show your progress in the app. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Cycle tracking

If you turn on cycle tracking, the period start dates you log are stored only on your phone and used only to show predictions and tips in the app. They are never shared or used for advertising. If you ask the AI coach a question, your current cycle day and phase are included so it can answer in context.

Family members

Everyone added under People shares this phone. Their data is stored the same way as yours and is deleted when you remove them.

Photos and the AI coach

Meal photos and coach questions are sent to the server and to Anthropic's Claude API to be analysed, then discarded. They are not stored or used for advertising.

Food search and barcodes

Online food search and barcode lookups query Open Food Facts. Only the search words or barcode number are sent.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
