Privacy Policy

W is a calorie, training and nutrition app. This page explains what data it uses and why.

Data on your phone

There are no accounts. Your profile (including any profile picture), food log, workouts, weigh-ins and water are stored only on your phone. A backup file is made only when you choose Back up to a file, and goes wherever you save or share it.

Cycle tracking

If you turn on cycle tracking, the period start dates you log are stored only on your phone and used only to show predictions and tips in the app. They are never shared or used for advertising.

Family members

Everyone added under People shares this phone. Their data is stored the same way as yours and is deleted when you remove them.

Food search and barcodes

Online food search and barcode lookups query Open Food Facts. Only the search words or barcode number are sent.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
