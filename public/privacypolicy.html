Privacy Policy

W is a calorie, training and nutrition app. This page explains what data it uses and why.

Data on your phone

Your profile, food log, workouts, weigh-ins, water and steps are stored on your phone. Without an account they never leave it.

Accounts and sync

If you create an account (email, Apple or Google), the same data is stored on the app's server so it can sync to your other phones. You can delete your account and all its data from Profile at any time.

Health data

If you allow it, the app reads your step count and weight from Apple Health or Health Connect, and saves weigh-ins you log back to them. Health data is used only to show your progress in the app. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Photos and the AI coach

Meal photos and coach questions are sent to the server and to Anthropic's Claude API to be analysed, then discarded. They are not stored or used for advertising.

Food search and barcodes

Online food search and barcode lookups query Open Food Facts. Only the search words or barcode number are sent.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub page.
